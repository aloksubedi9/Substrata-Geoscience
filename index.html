Why this combination

Geophysics tells you the shape of the problem. Monitoring tells you how fast it's changing.

Most firms specialise in one or the other — geophysical survey houses that hand over a report, or instrumentation vendors that sell hardware. We combine subsurface imaging with the point-sensor data needed to validate it, because a resistivity model without ground-truthed monitoring is a guess, and a piezometer reading without spatial context is a single point on an unknown surface.
